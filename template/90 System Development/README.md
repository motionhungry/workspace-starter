# System Development

Develop the workspace through actual use. Capture improvements, agree on what to change, test the change against observable criteria, and retain findings that will help a later session.

Open the [Development Index](Development%20Index.base) in Obsidian to see open tickets, items awaiting validation, and accepted decisions.

- `10 Improvements/` holds proposals and ongoing changes.
- `20 Decisions/` records lasting choices and their rationale.
- `30 Journal/` preserves concise findings from substantive sessions.
- `99 Archive/` retains inactive records and their stable IDs.

See [record conventions](AGENTS.md) and the [system-development skill](../.agents/skills/system-development/SKILL.md).
